# Incident Response Policy  
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Annex A.5.24

---

# 1. Purpose  
To define NovaPay’s approach to detecting, reporting, responding to, and recovering from information security incidents.

---

# 2. Scope  
This policy applies to:

- All NovaPay employees and contractors  
- All NovaPay systems and applications  
- All cloud environments (AWS/Azure)  
- All security monitoring tools (SIEM, EDR, WAF, IAM)  

---

# 3. Objectives  
NovaPay aims to:

- Detect incidents quickly  
- Respond effectively and consistently  
- Minimize business impact  
- Preserve forensic evidence  
- Prevent recurrence  
- Comply with ISO 27001, GDPR, and contractual obligations  

---

# 4. Definitions  

### Security Event  
Any observable occurrence in a system or network.

### Security Incident  
An event that compromises confidentiality, integrity, or availability.

### Major Incident  
An incident causing significant business disruption or regulatory impact.

---

# 5. Roles & Responsibilities  

### SOC Team  
- Monitor systems  
- Investigate alerts  
- Escalate incidents  
- Maintain incident logs  

### Incident Manager  
- Coordinates response  
- Approves containment actions  
- Communicates with management  

### IT Operations  
- Supports containment and recovery  
- Applies patches and fixes  

### Employees  
- Report incidents immediately  
- Follow response instructions  

---

# 6. Incident Reporting  
Incidents must be reported via:

- SOC hotline  
- Incident ticketing system  
- Email to security@novapay.com  

Reports must include:

- Description  
- Time  
- System affected  
- Evidence  

---

# 7. Incident Response Phases  

### 7.1 Identification  
Detect and validate the incident.

### 7.2 Containment  
Short‑term: stop the spread  
Long‑term: prevent recurrence

### 7.3 Eradication  
Remove malware, disable accounts, patch vulnerabilities.

### 7.4 Recovery  
Restore systems, validate integrity, monitor for recurrence.

### 7.5 Lessons Learned  
Document findings, update controls, improve processes.

---

# 8. Communication  
Major incidents require:

- Notification to senior management  
- Notification to affected customers (if applicable)  
- Regulatory reporting (GDPR 72‑hour rule)

---

# 9. Evidence Handling  
- Preserve logs  
- Capture system snapshots  
- Store evidence securely  
- Maintain chain of custody  

---

# 10. Review  
This policy must be reviewed:

- Annually  
- After major incidents  
- Before certification audits
