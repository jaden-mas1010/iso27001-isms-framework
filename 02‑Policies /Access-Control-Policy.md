# Access Control Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Access Control Policy defines the requirements for managing access to information 
systems, applications, and data. It ensures that access is granted based on business 
needs, least privilege, and security principles aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and temporary staff  
- All organisational information systems and applications  
- All devices accessing organisational data  
- All third‑party service providers with system access  

---

### 3. Access Control Principles

#### **Least Privilege**
Users are granted the minimum level of access required to perform their duties.

#### **Need-to-Know**
Access to sensitive information is restricted to authorised personnel only.

#### **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**
Access is assigned based on job roles and responsibilities.

#### **Segregation of Duties**
Critical tasks are divided to prevent fraud, error, or misuse.

---

### 4. User Access Management

#### **4.1 User Registration**
All user accounts must be formally requested, approved, and documented.

#### **4.2 User Provisioning**
Access rights must be assigned according to defined roles and responsibilities.

#### **4.3 User Deprovisioning**
Access must be removed immediately when employment or contract ends.

#### **4.4 Periodic Access Review**
Access rights must be reviewed at least annually to ensure accuracy.

---

### 5. Authentication Requirements

- Multi‑Factor Authentication (MFA) must be used for all critical systems.  
- Passwords must meet complexity requirements and be changed regularly.  
- Default credentials must be disabled or changed before deployment.  
- Shared accounts are prohibited except for approved service accounts.  

---

### 6. Privileged Access Management (PAM)

- Administrative access must be tightly controlled and monitored.  
- Privileged accounts must use MFA and secure authentication methods.  
- Privileged actions must be logged and reviewed regularly.  
- Privileged accounts must not be used for daily tasks.  

---

### 7. Remote Access

- Remote access must use secure VPN or encrypted channels.  
- Remote sessions must be monitored and logged.  
- Remote access is granted only when justified and approved.  

---

### 8. Access to Cloud Services

- Cloud access must follow the same RBAC and MFA requirements.  
- API keys, tokens, and secrets must be securely stored and rotated.  
- Cloud roles must be reviewed regularly for least privilege compliance.  

---

### 9. Third‑Party Access

- Third‑party access must be approved, documented, and time‑limited.  
- Suppliers must meet contractual security requirements.  
- Third‑party access must be monitored and revoked when no longer needed.  

---

### 10. Monitoring & Logging

- All access to critical systems must be logged.  
- Logs must be protected from tampering.  
- Access anomalies must trigger alerts and be investigated.  

---

### 11. Violations
Unauthorised access or misuse may result in:
- Disciplinary action  
- Revocation of access  
- Contract termination  
- Legal consequences  

---

### 12. Review
This policy will be reviewed annually or after major changes to systems or roles.

---

### 13. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
