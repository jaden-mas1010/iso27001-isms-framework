# Data Protection & Privacy Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Data Protection & Privacy Policy defines the requirements for collecting, 
processing, storing, transmitting, and disposing of personal data. It ensures compliance 
with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, GDPR, and other applicable privacy regulations.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All personal data processed by the organisation  
- All employees, contractors, and third‑party service providers  
- All systems, applications, and cloud services handling personal data  
- All processing activities including collection, storage, transfer, and deletion  

---

### 3. Definitions

#### **Personal Data**
Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

#### **Special Category Data**
Sensitive data requiring enhanced protection (e.g., health, biometrics).

#### **Processing**
Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, deletion).

#### **Data Subject**
An individual whose personal data is processed.

---

### 4. Data Protection Principles
The organisation follows these principles:

- **Lawfulness, Fairness, Transparency**  
- **Purpose Limitation**  
- **Data Minimisation**  
- **Accuracy**  
- **Storage Limitation**  
- **Integrity & Confidentiality**  
- **Accountability**  

---

### 5. Lawful Basis for Processing
Personal data must be processed only when a lawful basis exists, such as:
- Consent  
- Contractual necessity  
- Legal obligation  
- Vital interests  
- Legitimate interests  
- Public interest (where applicable)

---

### 6. Data Collection & Use
- Only the minimum necessary personal data may be collected.  
- Data must be used only for specified, legitimate purposes.  
- Data subjects must be informed about processing activities.  
- Automated decision‑making must follow regulatory requirements.

---

### 7. Data Storage & Protection
- Personal data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.  
- Access must follow least privilege and RBAC.  
- Logs must record access to sensitive personal data.  
- Personal data must not be stored in unsecured locations or personal devices.

---

### 8. Data Retention & Disposal
- Retention periods must be defined for each data category.  
- Personal data must not be kept longer than necessary.  
- Disposal must use secure deletion or destruction methods.  
- Retention schedules must be reviewed annually.

---

### 9. Data Subject Rights
The organisation must support the following rights:
- Right to access  
- Right to rectification  
- Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”)  
- Right to restrict processing  
- Right to data portability  
- Right to object  
- Rights related to automated decision‑making  

Requests must be handled promptly and documented.

---

### 10. Data Breach Management
- Personal data breaches must be reported immediately.  
- Breaches must be assessed for regulatory notification requirements.  
- Notifications to authorities or individuals must follow legal timelines.  
- Breach handling must follow the Incident Response Policy.

---

### 11. Third‑Party Processing
- Third‑party processors must meet contractual privacy requirements.  
- Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) must be in place.  
- Processors must implement adequate security controls.  
- Cross‑border transfers must follow legal requirements (e.g., SCCs).

---

### 12. Privacy by Design & Default
- New systems must undergo privacy impact assessments (DPIAs).  
- Default settings must minimise data exposure.  
- Designs must incorporate encryption, minimisation, and access controls.

---

### 13. Training & Awareness
- All personnel must receive privacy training annually.  
- High‑risk roles require additional training.  
- Training must cover GDPR, data handling, and breach reporting.

---

### 14. Compliance
Non‑compliance may result in:
- Disciplinary action  
- Revocation of access  
- Contract termination (for third parties)  
- Regulatory penalties  

---

### 15. Review
This policy will be reviewed annually or after major regulatory or organisational changes.

---

### 16. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
