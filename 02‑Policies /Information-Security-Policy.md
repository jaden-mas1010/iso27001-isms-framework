# Information Security Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Information Security Policy establishes the organisation’s commitment to protecting 
information assets and defines the principles, responsibilities, and expectations for 
maintaining confidentiality, integrity, and availability in accordance with ISO/IEC 
27001:2022.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and temporary staff  
- All information systems, applications, and infrastructure  
- All organisational data, regardless of format or location  
- All third‑party service providers handling organisational information  

---

### 3. Policy Statement
The organisation is committed to:
- Protecting information assets from unauthorised access, disclosure, modification, or loss  
- Implementing risk‑based security controls aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022  
- Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations  
- Maintaining secure operations through documented policies and procedures  
- Continually improving the Information Security Management System (ISMS)  

---

### 4. Information Security Principles
The organisation follows these principles:

#### **Confidentiality**
Information is accessible only to authorised individuals.

#### **Integrity**
Information is accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorised modification.

#### **Availability**
Information and systems are accessible when required to support business operations.

---

### 5. Responsibilities

#### **Senior Management**
- Approves the ISMS and ensures adequate resources  
- Supports security initiatives and risk management  

#### **ISMS Manager**
- Oversees ISMS implementation and documentation  
- Ensures compliance with policies and controls  

#### **IT & Security Team**
- Implements technical controls, monitoring, and incident response  
- Maintains secure configurations and infrastructure  

#### **Employees & Contractors**
- Follow all security policies and procedures  
- Report incidents or suspicious activity immediately  
- Complete mandatory security awareness training  

#### **Third‑Party Providers**
- Must comply with contractual and security requirements  
- Must protect organisational information appropriately  

---

### 6. Security Requirements
The organisation enforces the following requirements:

- Strong authentication and access control  
- Secure configuration and patch management  
- Logging, monitoring, and incident response  
- Data classification and secure handling  
- Backup and recovery processes  
- Supplier and cloud security management  
- Regular risk assessments and internal audits  

---

### 7. Policy Compliance
Non‑compliance with this policy may result in:
- Disciplinary action  
- Revocation of system access  
- Contract termination (for third parties)  

---

### 8. Review & Maintenance
This policy will be reviewed:
- Annually  
- After major organisational or technological changes  
- Following significant security incidents  

---

### 9. Approval
This policy is approved by senior management and forms a core component of the ISMS.
