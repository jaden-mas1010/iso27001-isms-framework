# Asset Management Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Asset Management Policy defines the requirements for identifying, classifying, 
protecting, and managing information assets throughout their lifecycle. It ensures 
assets are handled securely and consistently in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All information assets owned or managed by the organisation  
- All employees, contractors, and third‑party providers  
- All systems, applications, devices, and data repositories  
- All environments (on‑premises, cloud, hybrid)

---

### 3. Asset Ownership
- Every information asset must have a designated **Asset Owner**.  
- Asset Owners are responsible for classification, protection, and access approval.  
- Ownership must be documented in the Asset Inventory.

---

### 4. Asset Inventory
- All assets must be recorded in a central Asset Inventory.  
- Inventory must include:  
  - Asset name  
  - Type  
  - Owner  
  - Location  
  - Classification  
  - Dependencies  
- Inventory must be reviewed at least annually.

---

### 5. Asset Classification
Assets must be classified based on sensitivity and business impact:

- **Public** – No confidentiality concerns  
- **Internal** – General business information  
- **Confidential** – Sensitive operational information  
- **Restricted** – Highly sensitive or regulated information  

Classification determines handling, storage, and access requirements.

---

### 6. Acceptable Use
- Assets must be used only for authorised business purposes.  
- Misuse, unauthorised access, or tampering is prohibited.  
- Users must follow all security policies and procedures.

---

### 7. Handling Requirements

#### **Confidential & Restricted Assets**
- Must be encrypted at rest and in transit  
- Must be stored in secure locations  
- Must be accessed only by authorised personnel  
- Must be protected against unauthorised disclosure  

#### **Physical Assets**
- Must be stored securely when unattended  
- Must be protected against theft, loss, or damage  

---

### 8. Asset Lifecycle Management

#### **Acquisition**
- New assets must be assessed for security requirements.  
- Cloud and third‑party assets must undergo supplier evaluation.

#### **Use**
- Assets must be used according to classification and acceptable use rules.

#### **Transfer**
- Transfers must use secure channels and be approved by the Asset Owner.

#### **Disposal**
- Assets must be securely wiped, destroyed, or sanitised before disposal.  
- Disposal must be documented.

---

### 9. Third‑Party Assets
- Third‑party access to organisational assets must be approved and monitored.  
- Suppliers must meet contractual and security requirements.  
- Shared assets must be protected according to classification.

---

### 10. Monitoring & Review
- Asset usage must be monitored for anomalies.  
- Inventory and classifications must be reviewed annually.  
- Changes in ownership or classification must be updated promptly.

---

### 11. Compliance
Non‑compliance may result in:
- Disciplinary action  
- Revocation of access  
- Contract termination  
- Legal consequences  

---

### 12. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
