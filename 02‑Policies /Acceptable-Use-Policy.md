# Acceptable Use Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy defines the responsibilities and acceptable behaviours for 
employees, contractors, and third‑party personnel when using organisational systems, 
devices, networks, and information assets. It ensures secure and responsible use in 
alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, temporary staff, and third‑party users  
- All organisational systems, applications, networks, and cloud services  
- All devices including laptops, desktops, mobile devices, and removable media  
- All information assets regardless of classification  

---

### 3. General Responsibilities
Users must:
- Protect organisational information and systems  
- Use assets only for authorised business purposes  
- Follow all security policies and procedures  
- Report security incidents or suspicious activity immediately  
- Maintain the confidentiality of sensitive information  

---

### 4. Acceptable Use Requirements

#### **4.1 System & Device Use**
Users must:
- Use organisational devices responsibly  
- Lock screens when unattended  
- Keep devices updated with security patches  
- Use only approved software and applications  
- Store data only in authorised locations  

#### **4.2 Network Use**
Users must:
- Connect only to authorised networks  
- Use VPN for remote access  
- Avoid bypassing security controls or firewalls  
- Refrain from scanning or probing networks without approval  

#### **4.3 Email & Communication**
Users must:
- Use professional and appropriate communication  
- Avoid sharing sensitive data without encryption  
- Not open suspicious links or attachments  
- Report phishing attempts immediately  

---

### 5. Prohibited Activities
Users must NOT:
- Access systems or data without authorisation  
- Share passwords or authentication tokens  
- Install unauthorised software or tools  
- Disable or bypass security controls  
- Use organisational assets for illegal or harmful activities  
- Store sensitive data on personal devices  
- Engage in harassment, discrimination, or offensive behaviour online  
- Use organisational systems for cryptocurrency mining or similar activities  

---

### 6. Personal Use
Limited personal use of organisational systems is permitted if:
- It does not interfere with business operations  
- It does not violate any security policies  
- It does not involve inappropriate or illegal content  
- It does not introduce security risks  

---

### 7. Removable Media
- Use of USB drives or external media must be approved.  
- Removable media must be encrypted.  
- Media must be scanned for malware before use.  

---

### 8. Data Handling Requirements
Users must:
- Follow data classification and handling rules  
- Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit  
- Dispose of documents securely  
- Avoid copying sensitive data unnecessarily  

---

### 9. Remote Work Requirements
Remote users must:
- Use secure, private networks  
- Keep devices physically protected  
- Follow VPN and MFA requirements  
- Avoid discussing sensitive information in public spaces  

---

### 10. Monitoring & Logging
- User activity may be monitored for security and compliance.  
- Logs may be reviewed during investigations.  
- Monitoring will follow legal and regulatory requirements.  

---

### 11. Violations
Violations of this policy may result in:
- Revocation of access  
- Disciplinary action  
- Contract termination  
- Legal consequences  

---

### 12. Review
This policy will be reviewed annually or after major organisational or technological changes.

---

### 13. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
