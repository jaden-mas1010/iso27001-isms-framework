# Risk Management Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Risk Management Policy defines the organisation’s approach to identifying, 
assessing, treating, and monitoring information security risks. It ensures a consistent, 
structured, and repeatable risk management process aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All information assets, systems, applications, and services  
- All employees, contractors, and third‑party providers  
- All business processes within the ISMS scope  
- All environments (on‑premises, cloud, hybrid)

---

### 3. Risk Management Objectives
The organisation aims to:
- Identify risks that may impact confidentiality, integrity, or availability  
- Assess risks using a consistent methodology  
- Implement appropriate risk treatment options  
- Reduce risks to acceptable levels  
- Ensure continual monitoring and improvement  

---

### 4. Risk Assessment Methodology

#### **4.1 Identification**
Risks must be identified through:
- Asset inventories  
- Threat and vulnerability analysis  
- Incident trends  
- Supplier assessments  
- Changes in technology or operations  

#### **4.2 Analysis**
Each risk must be evaluated based on:
- **Likelihood** of occurrence  
- **Impact** on business operations  
- **Existing controls**  

#### **4.3 Evaluation**
Risks must be prioritised using a defined risk scoring model.  
Risks exceeding the organisation’s risk appetite must be treated.

---

### 5. Risk Treatment

Approved treatment options include:
- **Mitigation:** Implement controls to reduce likelihood or impact  
- **Avoidance:** Discontinue risky activities  
- **Transfer:** Use insurance or third‑party contracts  
- **Acceptance:** Approve risk when within tolerance  

Risk treatment plans must include:
- Selected controls  
- Responsible owners  
- Target completion dates  
- Residual risk evaluation  

---

### 6. Risk Acceptance
- Residual risks must be reviewed and approved by senior management.  
- Acceptance must be documented with justification.  
- High‑risk acceptance requires executive approval.

---

### 7. Monitoring & Review
- Risks must be reviewed at least annually.  
- Significant changes (new systems, suppliers, incidents) require reassessment.  
- Risk treatment progress must be monitored regularly.  
- Results must be reported during management review.

---

### 8. Documentation Requirements
The following must be maintained:
- Risk Register  
- Risk Assessment Reports  
- Risk Treatment Plans  
- Residual Risk Approvals  
- Evidence of control implementation  

---

### 9. Roles & Responsibilities

#### **ISMS Manager**
- Oversees risk management activities  
- Maintains the risk register  
- Ensures assessments follow methodology  

#### **Asset Owners**
- Identify risks related to their assets  
- Approve treatment plans  

#### **Senior Management**
- Approves high‑risk acceptance  
- Ensures resources for risk treatment  

---

### 10. Continuous Improvement
- Lessons learned from incidents and audits must inform risk updates.  
- Risk methodology must be reviewed annually.  
- Controls must be adjusted based on emerging threats.

---

### 11. Compliance
Non‑compliance may result in:
- Increased monitoring  
- Disciplinary action  
- Contract termination (for third parties)

---

### 12. Review
This policy will be reviewed annually or after major organisational or technological changes.

---

### 13. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
