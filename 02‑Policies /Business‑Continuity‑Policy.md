# Business Continuity Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Business Continuity Policy establishes the requirements for preparing, maintaining, 
and executing business continuity and disaster recovery capabilities. It ensures the 
organisation can continue critical operations during and after disruptive events in 
accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All critical business processes and supporting systems  
- All employees, contractors, and third‑party service providers  
- All organisational locations and cloud environments  
- All continuity and recovery planning activities  

---

### 3. Objectives
The organisation aims to:
- Maintain availability of critical services  
- Minimise operational disruption during incidents  
- Restore systems and processes within defined recovery timeframes  
- Protect essential data and infrastructure  
- Ensure coordinated response and communication  

---

### 4. Business Continuity Management (BCM)

#### **4.1 Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**
- Critical processes must be identified and assessed.  
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) must be defined.  
- Dependencies, risks, and impacts must be documented.

#### **4.2 Continuity Planning**
Plans must include:
- Roles and responsibilities  
- Communication procedures  
- Recovery strategies  
- Resource requirements  
- Escalation paths  

#### **4.3 Disaster Recovery (DR)**
DR plans must define:
- System recovery procedures  
- Backup and restoration processes  
- Failover and redundancy mechanisms  
- Cloud and infrastructure recovery steps  

---

### 5. Backup Requirements
- Critical data must be backed up regularly.  
- Backups must be encrypted and stored securely.  
- Backup integrity must be tested periodically.  
- Offsite or cloud backups must be maintained for resilience.

---

### 6. Testing & Exercises
- Business continuity and disaster recovery plans must be tested annually.  
- Tests may include tabletop exercises, simulations, or full failover tests.  
- Results must be documented and used to improve plans.

---

### 7. Communication & Coordination
During a disruption:
- The continuity team must coordinate response activities.  
- Employees must follow communication procedures.  
- External communication must be approved by senior management.  
- Customers and regulators must be notified when required.

---

### 8. Third‑Party Dependencies
- Critical suppliers must have adequate continuity capabilities.  
- Supplier continuity plans must be reviewed for high‑risk services.  
- Contracts must include continuity and recovery requirements.

---

### 9. Recovery & Restoration
- Systems must be restored according to defined RTO and RPO.  
- Data integrity must be validated after recovery.  
- Operations must resume in a controlled and secure manner.

---

### 10. Continuous Improvement
- Lessons learned from incidents and tests must be incorporated.  
- Plans must be updated after major organisational or technological changes.  
- BCM performance must be reviewed during management review.

---

### 11. Compliance
Non‑compliance may result in:
- Disciplinary action  
- Increased monitoring  
- Contract termination (for third parties)  

---

### 12. Review
This policy will be reviewed annually or after major changes to business operations or infrastructure.

---

### 13. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
