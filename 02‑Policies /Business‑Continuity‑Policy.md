# Business Continuity Policy  
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Annex A.5.29 & A.5.30

---

# 1. Purpose  
This Business Continuity Policy establishes NovaPay’s commitment to maintaining and restoring critical business operations during and after disruptive events. It ensures resilience, rapid recovery, and compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

# 2. Scope  
This policy applies to:

- All critical business processes and supporting systems  
- All employees, contractors, and third‑party service providers  
- All organisational locations and cloud environments  
- All continuity, disaster recovery, and resilience planning activities  

---

# 3. Policy Statement  
NovaPay is committed to:

- Ensuring continuity of essential services  
- Minimising operational disruption during incidents  
- Protecting critical data, infrastructure, and customer trust  
- Maintaining tested and effective continuity and recovery capabilities  
- Providing resources necessary for resilience and recovery  

Senior management supports and approves all business continuity and disaster recovery activities.

---

# 4. Business Continuity Management (BCM) Framework  

## 4.1 Business Impact Analysis (BIA)  
NovaPay must:

- Identify critical business processes  
- Define Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)  
- Define Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)  
- Document dependencies, risks, and impacts  
- Review BIA annually or after major changes  

### Critical Processes & Recovery Objectives

| Critical Process | RTO | RPO |
|------------------|------|------|
| Payment Processing | 2 hours | 15 minutes |
| Authentication Services | 1 hour | 5 minutes |
| Customer Support | 4 hours | 30 minutes |
| Cloud Infrastructure | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| SOC Monitoring | 30 minutes | 5 minutes |

---

## 4.2 Continuity Planning  
Continuity plans must include:

- Roles and responsibilities  
- Activation criteria  
- Communication procedures  
- Recovery strategies  
- Manual fallback procedures  
- Resource requirements  
- Escalation paths  

Plans must be reviewed annually.

---

## 4.3 Disaster Recovery (DR)  
DR plans must define:

- System recovery procedures  
- Backup and restoration processes  
- Failover and redundancy mechanisms  
- Cloud and infrastructure recovery steps  
- Validation of system integrity after restoration  

DR plans must align with RTO/RPO requirements.

---

# 5. Backup & Data Protection Requirements  
NovaPay must ensure:

- Regular backups of critical data  
- Encryption of all backup data  
- Secure offsite or cloud backup storage  
- Immutable backup storage for ransomware resilience  
- Quarterly backup integrity testing  
- Immediate escalation of backup failures  

---

# 6. Testing & Exercises  
NovaPay must test continuity and recovery capabilities:

- **Annually** – full BCP/DR simulation  
- **Quarterly** – partial or tabletop exercises  

Test results must be:

- Documented  
- Reviewed by the ISMS Manager  
- Used to improve continuity and recovery plans  

---

# 7. Communication & Coordination  
During a disruption:

- The Business Continuity Manager coordinates all response activities  
- Employees must follow defined communication procedures  
- External communication must be approved by senior management  
- Customers and regulators must be notified when required (e.g., GDPR 72‑hour rule)  

---

# 8. Third‑Party Dependencies  
NovaPay must ensure:

- Critical suppliers maintain adequate continuity capabilities  
- Supplier continuity plans are reviewed for high‑risk services  
- Contracts include continuity and recovery requirements  
- Supplier failures are incorporated into BIA and risk assessments  

---

# 9. Recovery & Restoration  
NovaPay must:

- Restore systems according to defined RTO/RPO  
- Validate data integrity after recovery  
- Resume operations in a controlled and secure manner  
- Document recovery actions and evidence  

---

# 10. Evidence & Record Keeping  
NovaPay must retain:

- BCP/DR test reports  
- Backup verification logs  
- Incident and disruption records  
- BIA documentation  
- Recovery evidence  

All records must be stored in the ISMS evidence repository.

---

# 11. Continuous Improvement  
NovaPay must:

- Incorporate lessons learned from incidents and tests  
- Update plans after major organisational or technological changes  
- Review BCM performance during management review  

---

# 12. Compliance  
Non‑compliance with this policy may result in:

- Disciplinary action  
- Increased monitoring  
- Contract termination (for third parties)  

---

# 13. Review  
This policy must be reviewed:

- Annually  
- After major organisational changes  
- Before certification audits  

---
