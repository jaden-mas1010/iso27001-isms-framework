# Physical Security Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Physical Security Policy defines the requirements for protecting organisational 
facilities, equipment, and physical information assets against unauthorised access, 
damage, theft, or disruption. It ensures compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 
physical security controls.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All organisational offices, facilities, and secure areas  
- All employees, contractors, visitors, and third‑party personnel  
- All physical information assets, equipment, and infrastructure  
- All environments including on‑premises, co‑location, and cloud‑connected facilities  

---

### 3. Physical Security Principles

#### **Protection of People and Assets**
Facilities must be designed and operated to protect personnel and information assets.

#### **Controlled Access**
Only authorised individuals may enter secure or restricted areas.

#### **Environmental Protection**
Facilities must be protected against fire, flood, temperature extremes, and other hazards.

#### **Equipment Security**
Devices and infrastructure must be protected from theft, tampering, and damage.

---

### 4. Facility Access Control

#### **4.1 Entry Controls**
- Access to facilities must require badges, keys, biometrics, or approved authentication.  
- Visitors must be registered, escorted, and logged.  
- Access logs must be retained and reviewed periodically.

#### **4.2 Secure Areas**
- Server rooms, network closets, and sensitive storage areas must be restricted.  
- Only authorised personnel may enter secure areas.  
- Access must be monitored using CCTV or equivalent controls.

---

### 5. Visitor Management
- Visitors must sign in and out.  
- Visitors must be escorted at all times.  
- Visitor badges must be visibly displayed.  
- Visitor access must be limited to non‑sensitive areas unless approved.

---

### 6. Equipment Security

#### **6.1 Workstations & Devices**
- Devices must be locked when unattended.  
- Equipment must be secured against theft (e.g., cable locks, locked cabinets).  
- Sensitive documents must not be left exposed.

#### **6.2 Servers & Network Equipment**
- Must be stored in secure, access‑controlled rooms.  
- Must be protected against tampering and environmental hazards.

#### **6.3 Portable Devices**
- Laptops, tablets, and removable media must be encrypted.  
- Loss or theft must be reported immediately.

---

### 7. Environmental Controls
Facilities must implement:
- Fire detection and suppression systems  
- Temperature and humidity monitoring  
- Flood and water damage protection  
- Uninterruptible power supply (UPS) and backup power  
- Protection against electrical surges  

---

### 8. Clear Desk & Clear Screen Requirements
- Sensitive documents must be stored securely when not in use.  
- Workstations must be locked when unattended.  
- Whiteboards containing sensitive information must be erased after meetings.

---

### 9. Physical Asset Disposal
- Paper documents must be shredded or securely destroyed.  
- Hard drives and media must be wiped or physically destroyed.  
- Disposal must be documented and approved.

---

### 10. Monitoring & Surveillance
- CCTV must be used in critical areas where legally permitted.  
- Surveillance footage must be retained for an appropriate period.  
- Physical access anomalies must be investigated.

---

### 11. Incident Reporting
Physical security incidents (e.g., theft, unauthorised entry, damage) must be reported 
immediately to the security team and handled under the Incident Response Policy.

---

### 12. Compliance
Non‑compliance may result in:
- Disciplinary action  
- Revocation of access  
- Contract termination (for third parties)  

---

### 13. Review
This policy will be reviewed annually or after major facility or infrastructure changes.

---

### 14. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
