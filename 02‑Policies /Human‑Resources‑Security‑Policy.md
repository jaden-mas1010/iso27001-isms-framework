# Human Resources Security Policy
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This Human Resources Security Policy defines the requirements for ensuring employees, 
contractors, and third‑party personnel understand and fulfil their information security 
responsibilities before, during, and after employment. It supports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 
Annex A controls related to personnel security.

---

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, temporary staff, and interns  
- All third‑party personnel with access to organisational systems or data  
- All HR processes including recruitment, onboarding, training, and termination  

---

### 3. Pre‑Employment Requirements

#### **3.1 Screening**
- Background checks must be performed where legally permitted.  
- Screening must be appropriate to the role and level of access.  
- Third‑party providers must ensure equivalent screening for their personnel.

#### **3.2 Contracts**
Employment and contractor agreements must include:
- Confidentiality obligations  
- Acceptable use requirements  
- Security responsibilities  
- Compliance expectations  

---

### 4. Onboarding Requirements

#### **4.1 Security Awareness**
All personnel must receive security awareness training covering:
- Information security principles  
- Acceptable use  
- Incident reporting  
- Data protection  
- Physical security  
- Secure handling of information assets  

Training must be completed before system access is granted.

#### **4.2 Role‑Based Responsibilities**
Personnel must understand:
- Their security responsibilities  
- Access privileges  
- Required procedures for their role  

---

### 5. During Employment

#### **5.1 Ongoing Training**
- Annual security awareness training is mandatory.  
- Additional training must be provided for high‑risk roles (e.g., developers, admins).

#### **5.2 Acceptable Use**
Personnel must comply with:
- Acceptable use policies  
- Access control requirements  
- Secure development and handling practices  

#### **5.3 Disciplinary Process**
Violations of security policies may result in:
- Formal warnings  
- Revocation of access  
- Suspension or termination  
- Legal action where applicable  

---

### 6. Termination or Role Change

#### **6.1 Access Revocation**
- Access must be removed immediately upon termination.  
- Accounts, badges, keys, and equipment must be returned.  
- Access changes must be documented.

#### **6.2 Exit Process**
Departing personnel must:
- Acknowledge ongoing confidentiality obligations  
- Return all organisational assets  
- Participate in exit interviews where applicable  

#### **6.3 Role Changes**
Access rights must be reviewed and adjusted when personnel change roles.

---

### 7. Third‑Party Personnel
- Third‑party staff must follow all organisational security policies.  
- Contracts must include security and confidentiality requirements.  
- Third‑party access must be monitored and reviewed regularly.

---

### 8. Reporting Security Incidents
Personnel must report:
- Suspicious activity  
- Policy violations  
- Security incidents  
- Loss or theft of equipment  

Reports must follow the Incident Response Policy.

---

### 9. Confidentiality & Ethics
Personnel must:
- Protect sensitive information  
- Avoid conflicts of interest  
- Follow ethical guidelines  
- Maintain professionalism and integrity  

---

### 10. Review
This policy will be reviewed annually or after major organisational or regulatory changes.

---

### 11. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
