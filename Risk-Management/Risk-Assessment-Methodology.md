# Risk Assessment Methodology
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Clause 6.1.2

This document defines NovaPay’s methodology for identifying, assessing, and treating information security risks.

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure risks are assessed consistently and objectively across NovaPay’s ISMS.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to:
- All information systems
- All departments
- All assets listed in the Asset Register
- All processes within ISMS scope

---

## 3. Risk Assessment Process

### Step 1 — Identify Assets  
Use the Asset Register to determine:
- Asset owner  
- Asset type  
- Classification  

### Step 2 — Identify Threats  
Examples:
- Malware  
- Insider misuse  
- Data leakage  
- Supplier failure  
- Unauthorized access  

### Step 3 — Identify Vulnerabilities  
Examples:
- Weak passwords  
- Missing patches  
- Misconfigurations  
- Lack of monitoring  

### Step 4 — Determine Likelihood  
Scale:
- 1 = Rare  
- 2 = Unlikely  
- 3 = Possible  
- 4 = Likely  
- 5 = Almost Certain  

### Step 5 — Determine Impact  
Scale:
- 1 = Negligible  
- 2 = Minor  
- 3 = Moderate  
- 4 = Major  
- 5 = Critical  

### Step 6 — Calculate Risk  
Formula:  
**Risk = Likelihood × Impact**

### Step 7 — Determine Treatment  
Options:
- Avoid  
- Reduce  
- Transfer  
- Accept  

---

## 4. Risk Acceptance Criteria

NovaPay accepts risks scoring:
- **1–6** (Low)  
- **7–12** (Medium) only with management approval  
- **13–25** (High/Critical) must be treated  

---

## 5. Review

Risk assessments are reviewed:
- Annually  
- After major changes  
- After incidents
