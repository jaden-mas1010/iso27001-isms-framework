# Risk Treatment Plan
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Clause 6.1.3

This plan defines how NovaPay treats identified information security risks.

---

## 1. Purpose

To document selected risk treatment options and assign responsibilities.

---

## 2. Treatment Options

- **Avoid:** Stop the activity causing the risk  
- **Reduce:** Implement controls to lower likelihood/impact  
- **Transfer:** Use insurance or suppliers  
- **Accept:** Approve residual risk  

---

---

## 4. Residual Risk

Residual risks must be:
- Documented  
- Approved by senior management  
- Reviewed annually
