# Asset Ownership  
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Annex A.5.9 & A.5.10

---

# 1. Purpose  
To define ownership responsibilities for NovaPay’s information assets and ensure accountability for protection, classification, and lifecycle management.

---

# 2. Scope  
This document applies to:

- All information assets listed in the Asset Inventory  
- All employees, contractors, and third‑party service providers  
- All cloud, hardware, software, and data assets  

---

# 3. Ownership Principles  

### 3.1 Every Asset Must Have an Owner  
Each asset must have a designated individual responsible for:

- Classification  
- Protection  
- Access control  
- Lifecycle management  
- Review and updates  

### 3.2 Ownership Does Not Mean Technical Control  
Owners define requirements; IT Ops, DevOps, or SOC may implement them.

### 3.3 Ownership Must Be Documented  
All ownership assignments must be recorded in the Asset Inventory.

---

# 4. Asset Owner Responsibilities  

### 4.1 Classification  
Owners must classify assets according to NovaPay’s Information Classification Policy.

### 4.2 Protection  
Owners ensure assets follow:

- Access control requirements  
- Encryption standards  
- Backup requirements  
- Logging & monitoring  

### 4.3 Access Management  
Owners approve:

- Access requests  
- Privileged access  
- Removal of access (JML process)

### 4.4 Lifecycle Management  
Owners ensure:

- Assets are updated  
- Assets are retired securely  
- Data is archived or destroyed properly  

### 4.5 Annual Review  
Owners must review their assets annually or after major changes.

---

# 6. Transfer of Ownership  
Ownership must be transferred when:

- Employees change roles  
- Systems are migrated  
- Assets are retired  
- New owners are assigned  

Transfers must be documented in the Asset Inventory.

---

# 7. Non‑Compliance  
Failure to fulfil ownership responsibilities may result in:

- Additional monitoring  
- Corrective actions  
- Escalation to senior management  

---

# 8. Review  
This document must be reviewed:

- Annually  
- After major organisational changes  
- Before certification audits
