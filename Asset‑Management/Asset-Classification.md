# Asset Classification
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – A.5.11

NovaPay classifies information assets to ensure appropriate protection based on sensitivity, business impact, and regulatory requirements.

---

## 1. Purpose

To define how assets are classified and ensure security controls match the sensitivity of the information.

---

## 2. Classification Levels

### 1. Public
Information approved for public release.
Examples:
- Marketing materials
- Public website content

### 2. Internal
Information intended for internal use only.
Examples:
- Internal documentation
- Non-sensitive operational data

### 3. Confidential
Information that could cause harm if disclosed.
Examples:
- Customer data
- Employee records
- Internal financial data

### 4. Highly Confidential
Information requiring the highest level of protection.
Examples:
- Encryption keys
- Security configurations
- Incident investigation data

---

## 3. Classification Process

1. Identify the asset  
2. Determine sensitivity  
3. Assign classification level  
4. Apply required controls  
5. Review classification annually  

---

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Asset Owners:** Assign classification  
- **ISMS Manager:** Ensure consistency  
- **Employees:** Handle assets according to classification  

---

## 5. Review

Asset classifications are reviewed:
- Annually  
- After major changes  
- After incidents
