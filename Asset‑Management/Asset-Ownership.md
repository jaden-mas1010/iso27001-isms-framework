# Asset Ownership
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – A.5.10

Each information asset must have a designated owner responsible for ensuring appropriate protection and lifecycle management.

---

## 1. Purpose

To define ownership responsibilities for NovaPay’s information assets.

---

## 2. Asset Owner Responsibilities

Asset owners must:
- Ensure correct classification  
- Approve access requests  
- Maintain asset accuracy  
- Ensure controls are applied  
- Review access rights regularly  
- Support risk assessments  
- Ensure secure disposal  

---

## 3. Asset Types & Owners

| Asset Type | Examples | Owner |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Data Assets | Customer data, logs | Data Protection Officer |
| Technical Assets | Servers, databases | IT Operations |
| Application Assets | Web apps, APIs | Development Lead |
| Physical Assets | Laptops, devices | Facilities Manager |
| Security Assets | SIEM, EDR | SOC Manager |

---

## 4. Ownership Transfer

Ownership must be updated when:
- Roles change  
- Departments restructure  
- Systems are replaced  

---

## 5. Review

Ownership is reviewed:
- Annually  
- During audits  
- After major organizational changes
