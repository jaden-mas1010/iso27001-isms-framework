# Statement of Applicability (SOA)
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022

## 1. Purpose
The Statement of Applicability (SOA) defines:
- Which Annex A controls are applicable to NovaPay
- Why each control is included or excluded
- The implementation status of each control
- The reference documents providing evidence

This is a mandatory ISO 27001 document required for certification.

---

## 2. Scope
This SOA applies to:
- All NovaPay systems
- All employees and contractors
- All cloud environments
- All data processing activities
- All third‑party suppliers

---

## 3. Methodology
Controls are evaluated based on:
- Risk Assessment results  
- Legal and regulatory requirements  
- Business needs  
- Contractual obligations  
- Technical architecture  

Each control is marked:
- **Applicable**  
- **Not Applicable** (with justification)

Status:
- Implemented  
- Partially Implemented  
- Not Implemented
