# Internal Audit Plan  
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Clause 9.2

---

# 1. Purpose  
To define NovaPay’s internal audit approach, schedule, responsibilities, and methodology to ensure the ISMS is effectively implemented and maintained.

---

# 2. Audit Objectives  
Internal audits aim to:

- Verify conformity with ISO/IEC 27001:2022  
- Confirm implementation of Annex A controls  
- Validate effectiveness of policies and procedures  
- Identify non‑conformities and improvement opportunities  
- Ensure readiness for external certification audits  

---

# 3. Audit Scope  
The scope includes:

- All ISMS documentation  
- All Annex A controls  
- All departments handling NovaPay information  
- Cloud environments (AWS/Azure)  
- Incident response processes  
- Supplier management  
- Business continuity  
- Risk management  

---

# 4. Audit Criteria  
Audits will be performed against:

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standard  
- NovaPay policies and procedures  
- Legal and regulatory requirements (GDPR, DPA 2018)  
- Contractual obligations  
- Internal security requirements  

---

# 5. Audit Frequency  
NovaPay conducts:

- **Annual full ISMS audit**  
- **Quarterly focused audits** (technical controls, suppliers, incidents)  
- **Ad‑hoc audits** after major incidents or changes  

---

# 6. Audit Schedule (Annual)

| Quarter | Audit Focus | Auditor | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|---------|----------|
| Q1 | Policies, Procedures, Annex A | ISMS Manager | Document Review |
| Q2 | Technical Controls (A.8) | Security Lead | Logs, Configs |
| Q3 | Supplier Security (A.5.19–A.5.23) | Procurement + ISMS | Supplier Records |
| Q4 | Business Continuity & Incident Response | IT Ops + ISMS | BCP Test Results |

---

# 7. Audit Methodology  
Audits follow these steps:

1. **Planning**  
   - Define scope, criteria, and evidence  
2. **Execution**  
   - Interviews  
   - Document review  
   - Technical verification  
3. **Reporting**  
   - Non‑conformities  
   - Observations  
   - Opportunities for improvement  
4. **Corrective Actions**  
   - Root cause analysis  
   - Remediation plan  
5. **Follow‑up**  
   - Verify closure of actions  

---

# 8. Roles & Responsibilities  

### ISMS Manager  
- Oversees audit program  
- Ensures independence of auditors  
- Reviews audit results  

### Internal Auditor  
- Conducts audits  
- Documents findings  
- Ensures objectivity  

### Department Heads  
- Provide evidence  
- Implement corrective actions  

---

# 9. Audit Evidence  
Evidence may include:

- Policies and procedures  
- Logs and monitoring reports  
- Access reviews  
- Supplier contracts  
- Incident reports  
- BCP test results  
- Risk register updates  

---

# 10. Review  
This audit plan must be reviewed annually or after major organizational changes.
