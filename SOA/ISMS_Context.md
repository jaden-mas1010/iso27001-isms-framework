# ISMS Context  
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Clause 4

---

# 1. Understanding the Organization and Its Context
NovaPay operates as a digital financial services platform providing secure payment processing, customer account management, and cloud‑based financial applications.

Key internal factors:
- Cloud‑native architecture (AWS/Azure)
- Distributed workforce (remote + on‑site)
- High dependency on third‑party SaaS tools
- Rapid development cycles (DevOps)

Key external factors:
- Regulatory requirements (GDPR, DPA 2018)
- Customer expectations for security and uptime
- Evolving cyber threats (phishing, malware, cloud attacks)
- Supplier dependencies (cloud hosting, payment gateways)

---

# 2. Understanding the Needs and Expectations of Interested Parties
Interested parties include:

### Internal
- Employees  
- Senior management  
- Development teams  
- SOC / Security team  

### External
- Customers  
- Regulators  
- Cloud service providers  
- Third‑party suppliers  
- Auditors  

Their expectations include:
- Confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data  
- Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements  
- Secure processing of financial transactions  
- Transparency during incidents  
- Reliable service uptime  

---

# 3. Scope of the ISMS
The ISMS covers:

- All NovaPay information systems  
- All cloud environments (AWS/Azure)  
- All production applications  
- All employees and contractors  
- All data processing activities  
- All third‑party integrations  
- All physical and virtual infrastructure supporting NovaPay services  

### Exclusions
- Personal devices not used for NovaPay work  
- Non‑production test environments without customer data  

---

# 4. ISMS Boundaries
The ISMS boundary includes:

- Corporate office locations  
- Remote working environments  
- Cloud infrastructure  
- Network infrastructure  
- Application development lifecycle  
- Supplier relationships  
- Incident response processes  

The boundary does **not** include:
- Personal social media accounts  
- Non‑NovaPay personal devices  
- External systems not controlled by NovaPay  

---

# 5. ISMS Objectives
NovaPay’s ISMS objectives are:

1. Protect customer data from unauthorized access  
2. Maintain service availability at 99.9% uptime  
3. Ensure compliance with GDPR and ISO 27001  
4. Reduce security incidents by 30% annually  
5. Strengthen supplier security through annual reviews  
6. Improve SOC detection and response capabilities  

Objectives are reviewed annually.

---

# 6. ISMS Integration with Business Processes
The ISMS is integrated into:

- DevOps pipelines  
- Change management  
- Incident response  
- Supplier onboarding  
- Risk assessment  
- Business continuity planning
