# Supplier Security Policy  
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Annex A.5.19 – A.5.22

---

# 1. Purpose  
This policy ensures that all suppliers, service providers, and third parties supporting NovaPay meet required security, continuity, and compliance standards.

---

# 2. Scope  
This policy applies to:

- All suppliers providing services to NovaPay  
- All third‑party platforms, SaaS tools, and cloud providers  
- All outsourced operational, technical, or security functions  
- All supplier onboarding, monitoring, and offboarding activities  

---

# 3. Policy Statement  
NovaPay is committed to:

- Assessing supplier risks before onboarding  
- Ensuring suppliers meet security and compliance requirements  
- Monitoring supplier performance and security posture  
- Maintaining continuity and resilience for critical suppliers  
- Enforcing contractual security obligations  

---

# 4. Supplier Classification  
Suppliers are classified based on risk:

### 4.1 Critical Suppliers  
Provide essential services required for NovaPay operations.  
Examples: Cloud provider, payment gateway, authentication provider.

### 4.2 High‑Risk Suppliers  
Process sensitive data or impact security.  
Examples: SOC tools, logging platforms, email provider.

### 4.3 Standard Suppliers  
Low‑risk operational or administrative services.  
Examples: HR tools, documentation platforms.

---

# 5. Supplier Security Requirements  

### 5.1 Security Controls  
Suppliers must implement:

- Access control  
- Encryption  
- Logging and monitoring  
- Vulnerability management  
- Incident response  

### 5.2 Compliance Requirements  
Critical suppliers must comply with:

- ISO 27001  
- SOC 2  
- GDPR  
- PCI‑DSS (if applicable)

### 5.3 Contractual Requirements  
Contracts must include:

- Security obligations  
- Data protection clauses  
- Breach notification timelines  
- Right to audit (for critical suppliers)  

---

# 6. Supplier Risk Assessment  
Before onboarding, NovaPay must:

- Perform a supplier risk assessment  
- Review security certifications  
- Evaluate data handling practices  
- Assess continuity and DR capabilities  
- Document risk level and approval  

Risk assessments must be reviewed annually.

---

# 7. Supplier Monitoring  
NovaPay must:

- Monitor supplier performance  
- Review security posture annually  
- Track incidents involving suppliers  
- Validate compliance certifications  
- Update supplier risk ratings  

---

# 8. Supplier Offboarding  
When terminating a supplier:

- Access must be revoked  
- Data must be securely returned or deleted  
- Accounts must be disabled  
- Risks must be reassessed  
- Documentation must be archived  

---

# 9. Non‑Compliance  
If a supplier fails to meet requirements:

- Additional controls may be required  
- Contract may be suspended  
- Supplier may be replaced  
- Incident response may be triggered  

---

# 10. Review  
This policy must be reviewed:

- Annually  
- After major supplier changes  
- Before certification audits
