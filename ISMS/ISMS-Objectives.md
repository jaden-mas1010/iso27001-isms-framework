# ISMS Objectives
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This document defines the Information Security Management System (ISMS) objectives 
aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.2. These objectives support the organisation’s 
strategic direction and ensure the protection of information assets.

---

### 2. Principles
The ISMS objectives are based on the following principles:

- Confidentiality: Prevent unauthorised access to information  
- Integrity: Ensure accuracy, completeness, and reliability of information  
- Availability: Ensure information and systems are accessible when required  
- Compliance: Meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations  
- Risk Management: Reduce information security risks to acceptable levels  

---

### 3. ISMS Objectives
The organisation has established the following measurable ISMS objectives:

#### **Objective 1 — Strengthen Access Control**
Implement and maintain secure access mechanisms to ensure only authorised users can 
access information systems.
- **Target:** 100% of systems protected by MFA and role‑based access control  
- **Measure:** Access control audit results  
- **Timeline:** Annual review  

---

#### **Objective 2 — Improve Incident Detection & Response**
Enhance monitoring and response capabilities to reduce the impact of security incidents.
- **Target:** Detect and respond to incidents within defined SLAs  
- **Measure:** Incident response metrics and logs  
- **Timeline:** Quarterly review  

---

#### **Objective 3 — Maintain Compliance with ISO/IEC 27001**
Ensure all applicable Annex A controls are implemented and maintained.
- **Target:** 100% control coverage in the Statement of Applicability  
- **Measure:** Internal audit results  
- **Timeline:** Annual audit cycle  

---

#### **Objective 4 — Strengthen Supplier & Cloud Security**
Ensure third‑party providers meet security requirements.
- **Target:** 100% of suppliers evaluated annually  
- **Measure:** Supplier risk assessments  
- **Timeline:** Annual review  

---

#### **Objective 5 — Improve Security Awareness**
Ensure all employees understand their security responsibilities.
- **Target:** 100% annual security awareness training completion  
- **Measure:** Training attendance records  
- **Timeline:** Annual training cycle  

---

### 4. Monitoring & Review
ISMS objectives will be:
- Monitored through KPIs  
- Reviewed during management review  
- Updated based on risk assessment outcomes  
- Adjusted when organisational or regulatory changes occur  

---

### 5. Approval
These objectives are approved by senior management and form a core part of the ISMS.
