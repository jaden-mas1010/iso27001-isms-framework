# ISMS Overview
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This document provides an overview of the organisation’s Information Security Management 
System (ISMS). It explains the ISMS structure, objectives, governance model, and alignment 
with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.

---

### 2. What the ISMS Is
The ISMS is a formal, risk‑based framework designed to protect the confidentiality, 
integrity, and availability of information assets. It establishes policies, procedures, 
controls, and governance processes to manage information security effectively.

The ISMS ensures:
- Risks are identified, assessed, and treated  
- Security controls are implemented and monitored  
- Compliance obligations are met  
- Incidents are detected and responded to  
- Continuous improvement is maintained  

---

### 3. ISMS Structure
The ISMS is organised into the following components:

- **ISMS Scope**  
  Defines boundaries, inclusions, and exclusions.

- **Context of the Organisation**  
  Identifies internal/external issues and strategic direction.

- **Interested Parties**  
  Defines stakeholders and their expectations.

- **ISMS Objectives**  
  Establishes measurable security goals.

- **Policies & Procedures**  
  Provide governance and operational guidance.

- **Annex A Controls**  
  Implement ISO/IEC 27002:2022 control guidance.

- **Risk Management**  
  Identifies and treats information security risks.

- **Internal Audit & Review**  
  Ensures compliance and continuous improvement.

---

### 4. Governance Model
The ISMS is governed through:

- Senior management oversight  
- ISMS Manager coordination  
- IT/Security team implementation  
- Departmental responsibility for local risks  
- Supplier and third‑party compliance  

Roles and responsibilities are defined in the ISMS governance documentation.

---

### 5. Alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022
The ISMS meets the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 by:

- Establishing a risk‑based security framework  
- Implementing Annex A controls  
- Maintaining documented policies and procedures  
- Ensuring monitoring, measurement, and reporting  
- Conducting internal audits and management reviews  
- Supporting continual improvement  

---

### 6. Continual Improvement
The ISMS follows a continual improvement cycle:

- Plan: Define scope, context, risks, and controls  
- Do: Implement policies, procedures, and controls  
- Check: Monitor, audit, and measure performance  
- Act: Improve based on findings and changes  

---

### 7. Conclusion
This overview summarises the structure and operation of the ISMS. It forms the foundation 
for all supporting documentation and demonstrates alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
