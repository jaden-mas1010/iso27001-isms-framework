# Interested Parties
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This document identifies the interested parties relevant to the organisation’s 
Information Security Management System (ISMS) and outlines their needs and expectations 
as required by ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 4.2.

---

### 2. Identification of Interested Parties
The following stakeholders have been identified as relevant to the ISMS:

- **Customers and Clients**  
  Rely on secure handling of their data and consistent service availability.

- **Employees and Contractors**  
  Require clear security policies, training, and defined responsibilities.

- **Senior Management**  
  Expect effective risk management, compliance, and operational resilience.

- **Regulatory Authorities**  
  Require adherence to legal, regulatory, and industry‑specific obligations.

- **Suppliers and Third‑Party Service Providers**  
  Must meet contractual and security requirements to protect shared data.

- **Cloud and Technology Providers**  
  Provide infrastructure and services that must be securely managed.

- **Auditors and Assessors**  
  Expect documented evidence of compliance and effective control implementation.

---

### 3. Needs and Expectations
The needs and expectations of each interested party include:

- Protection of personal, confidential, and business‑critical information  
- Compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and contractual obligations  
- Secure and reliable service delivery  
- Effective incident detection, response, and communication  
- Transparent governance and risk‑based security controls  
- Assurance of business continuity and resilience  
- Proper management of suppliers and external dependencies  

These expectations directly influence the selection and implementation of Annex A controls.

---

### 4. Impact on the ISMS
Understanding the needs of interested parties ensures that:

- Risks are assessed accurately  
- Controls are selected appropriately  
- Policies and procedures address real operational requirements  
- Compliance obligations are met  
- Stakeholder trust is maintained  

This information forms a core part of the ISMS foundation.

---

### 5. Review
The list of interested parties and their expectations will be reviewed annually or when 
significant organisational or regulatory changes occur.
