# ISMS Scope
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This document defines the scope and boundaries of the Information Security Management 
System (ISMS) implemented in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

### 2. Organizational Context
The ISMS applies to the information assets, processes, technologies, and personnel 
involved in the delivery, management, and protection of the organisation’s information 
services. The scope reflects internal operations, external dependencies, and regulatory 
requirements relevant to information security.

### 3. ISMS Boundaries
The ISMS covers:
- All information systems used for business operations  
- All employees, contractors, and third parties handling organisational data  
- All physical locations where information is stored or processed  
- All cloud services, hosted platforms, and external providers supporting operations  

### 4. Inclusions
The ISMS includes:
- Governance, risk management, and compliance activities  
- Technical and organisational security controls  
- Policies, procedures, and operational processes  
- Incident response and business continuity capabilities  
- Supplier and cloud security management  

### 5. Exclusions
The following areas are excluded from the ISMS scope:
- Personal devices not enrolled in organisational security controls  
- Third‑party systems not managed or controlled by the organisation  
(Exclusions must not reduce the effectiveness of the ISMS.)

### 6. Scope Statement
The ISMS covers all information assets, supporting technologies, personnel, and 
processes required to deliver and protect the organisation’s information services. 
It ensures confidentiality, integrity, and availability through risk‑based controls 
aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 27002:2022.

### 7. Approval
This scope is approved by senior management and forms the foundation of the ISMS.
