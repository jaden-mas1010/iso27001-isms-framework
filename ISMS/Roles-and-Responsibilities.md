# Roles and Responsibilities
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This document defines the roles and responsibilities required to establish, implement, 
maintain, and continually improve the Information Security Management System (ISMS) in 
accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 5.3.

---

### 2. Senior Management
Senior management is responsible for:
- Approving the ISMS scope, policies, and objectives  
- Ensuring resources are available for ISMS implementation  
- Supporting risk management and decision‑making  
- Reviewing ISMS performance and approving improvements  
- Demonstrating leadership and commitment to information security  

---

### 3. ISMS Manager
The ISMS Manager is responsible for:
- Coordinating ISMS activities and documentation  
- Managing risk assessments and treatment plans  
- Ensuring Annex A controls are implemented and maintained  
- Monitoring compliance with policies and procedures  
- Reporting ISMS performance to senior management  
- Managing internal audits and corrective actions  

---

### 4. IT & Security Team
The IT/Security team is responsible for:
- Implementing technical security controls  
- Managing access control, logging, monitoring, and patching  
- Responding to security incidents and vulnerabilities  
- Maintaining secure configurations and infrastructure  
- Supporting business continuity and disaster recovery  

---

### 5. Department Managers
Department managers are responsible for:
- Ensuring staff follow security policies and procedures  
- Identifying risks within their departments  
- Supporting incident reporting and response  
- Ensuring secure handling of information assets  

---

### 6. Employees & Contractors
All personnel are responsible for:
- Following information security policies and procedures  
- Protecting organisational information assets  
- Reporting security incidents or suspicious activity  
- Completing mandatory security awareness training  

---

### 7. External Providers
Suppliers and third‑party service providers are responsible for:
- Meeting contractual and security requirements  
- Protecting shared or processed information  
- Supporting audits or assessments when required  

---

### 8. Review
Roles and responsibilities will be reviewed annually or when organisational changes occur.
