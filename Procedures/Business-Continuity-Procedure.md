# Business Continuity Procedure

## Purpose
To ensure NovaPay can continue critical business operations during and after disruptive events such as cyberattacks, system failures, natural disasters, or major outages.

## Scope
All critical business processes, systems, applications, cloud services, and personnel required to maintain NovaPay’s operational continuity.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **BCP Manager** – Activates and coordinates the Business Continuity Plan.
- **IT Operations** – Restores systems and infrastructure.
- **Communications Lead** – Handles internal and external communication.
- **ISMS Manager** – Ensures alignment with ISO 27001 requirements.
- **System Owners** – Validate recovery of their respective systems.

---

## 1. Activation Criteria
The Business Continuity Plan (BCP) is activated when:
- Critical systems are unavailable for more than 30 minutes.
- A cyberattack disrupts operations.
- A major outage affects customer‑facing services.
- A disaster impacts NovaPay facilities or cloud regions.

The BCP Manager formally declares activation.

---

## 2. Communication
### Internal Communication
- Notify all employees via email or emergency channels.
- Provide clear instructions and status updates.

### External Communication
- Notify customers if services are impacted.
- Notify suppliers if dependencies are affected.
- Regulatory notification if required (e.g., GDPR breach).

All communication must be approved by the Communications Lead.

---

## 3. Recovery Actions
### 3.1 System Recovery
- Switch to backup systems or failover environments.
- Restore critical services first (payment systems, customer portals).
- Validate system integrity before returning to production.

### 3.2 Manual Workarounds
If systems are unavailable:
- Use documented manual processes.
- Log all manual transactions for later reconciliation.

### 3.3 Resource Allocation
- Assign staff to critical functions.
- Relocate operations if physical facilities are affected.

---

## 4. Return to Normal Operations
Once systems are stable:
- Restore primary systems.
- Validate full functionality.
- Reconcile any manual work performed.
- Notify staff and customers that normal operations have resumed.

---

## 5. Post‑Incident Review
Within 5 business days:
- Conduct a full review of the disruption.
- Identify root cause.
- Document lessons learned.
- Update BCP, procedures, and controls.
- Add corrective actions to the ISMS improvement log.

---

## 6. Testing Requirements
NovaPay must test the Business Continuity Plan:
- **Annually** for full BCP simulation.
- **Quarterly** for partial or tabletop exercises.

Test results must be documented and reviewed by the ISMS Manager.

---

## Review
This procedure must be reviewed annually or after any major disruption.
