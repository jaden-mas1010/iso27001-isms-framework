# Information Security Policy

## Purpose
To define NovaPay’s commitment to protecting information assets and maintaining an effective Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

## Objectives
- Protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.
- Ensure compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
- Manage risks through structured controls and continuous improvement.

## Scope
All employees, contractors, systems, data, and third parties.

## Policy
- Information security is a business priority.
- All staff must follow ISMS policies and procedures.
- Access is granted on a least‑privilege basis.
- Security incidents must be reported immediately.
- Risk assessments must be performed annually.
- Controls must align with ISO 27001 Annex A.

## Responsibilities
- ISMS Manager: Oversight and governance.
- Asset Owners: Protection of assigned assets.
- All Staff: Compliance with policies.

## Review
Annually or after major changes.
