# Risk Assessment Procedure

## Purpose
To define how NovaPay identifies, evaluates, and treats information security risks in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.2 and 6.1.3.

## Scope
All information assets, processes, systems, people, and third parties.

## Steps

### 1. Identify Assets
- Use the Asset Register (Asset_Register_NovaPay.xlsx)
- Assign owners
- Classify data (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted)

### 2. Identify Threats & Vulnerabilities
- Technical (malware, misconfigurations)
- Human (insider threat, error)
- Physical (theft, fire)
- Process (weak procedures)

### 3. Evaluate Risks
- Likelihood (1–5)
- Impact (1–5)
- Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

### 4. Select Treatment Options
- Avoid
- Reduce
- Transfer
- Accept

### 5. Document in Risk Register
- Add controls from Annex A
- Assign owners
- Set deadlines

### 6. Review Annually
Or after major changes or incidents.
