# Asset Management Procedure
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System

### 1. Purpose
This procedure defines how information assets are identified, classified, recorded, 
maintained, and protected throughout their lifecycle. It ensures compliance with 
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls related to asset management.

---

### 2. Scope
This procedure applies to:
- All information assets (data, systems, devices, software, cloud services)
- All employees, contractors, and third‑party personnel
- All environments (on‑premises, cloud, hybrid)

---

### 3. Asset Categories
Assets must be grouped into:
- Hardware (laptops, servers, mobile devices)
- Software (applications, licenses, SaaS)
- Information (documents, databases, records)
- Cloud services (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS)
- People (roles with access rights)
- Facilities (offices, server rooms)

---

### 4. Asset Inventory

#### **4.1 Creation**
All assets must be recorded in the Asset Register including:
- Asset name and type
- Owner
- Location
- Classification
- Serial number or identifier
- Supplier (if applicable)
- Status (active, retired)

#### **4.2 Ownership**
Each asset must have a designated **Asset Owner** responsible for:
- Authorising access
- Ensuring protection
- Approving changes
- Maintaining accuracy of records

#### **4.3 Updates**
The Asset Register must be updated when:
- New assets are acquired
- Assets are transferred
- Assets are retired or disposed
- Ownership changes

---

### 5. Asset Classification

Assets must be classified according to sensitivity:
- **Public**
- **Internal**
- **Confidential**
- **Restricted**

Classification determines:
- Access controls
- Storage requirements
- Encryption needs
- Handling procedures

---

### 6. Asset Handling Requirements

#### **6.1 Storage**
- Sensitive assets must be stored securely.
- Confidential/Restricted data must be encrypted.

#### **6.2 Transmission**
- Sensitive data must be encrypted in transit.
- Only approved communication channels may be used.

#### **6.3 Use**
- Users must follow acceptable use and security policies.
- Assets must not be used for unauthorised purposes.

---

### 7. Asset Lifecycle Management

#### **7.1 Acquisition**
- All new assets must be approved by the Asset Owner.
- Procurement must ensure security requirements are met.

#### **7.2 Maintenance**
- Software must be patched regularly.
- Hardware must be maintained according to vendor guidance.

#### **7.3 Transfer**
- Transfers must be documented.
- Access rights must be updated accordingly.

#### **7.4 Disposal**
Assets must be disposed securely:
- Data wiped or destroyed
- Hardware physically destroyed if required
- Disposal recorded in the Asset Register

---

### 8. Third‑Party Assets
- Third‑party assets must meet organisational security requirements.
- Contracts must define security responsibilities.
- Access to organisational data must be controlled and monitored.

---

### 9. Monitoring & Review
- Asset Register must be reviewed quarterly.
- Discrepancies must be investigated.
- Asset Owners must validate accuracy.

---

### 10. Non‑Compliance
Non‑compliance may result in:
- Revocation of access
- Disciplinary action
- Contract termination (for third parties)

---

### 11. Review
This procedure will be reviewed annually or after major organisational changes.

---

### 12. Approval
Approved by senior management as part of the ISMS governance framework.
