# Annex A.7 – Physical Controls  
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.7 contains 14 controls focused on physical protection of facilities, equipment, and environments.

---

## A.7.1 Physical Security Perimeters  
Sensitive areas (server rooms, network closets) must have restricted access, reinforced doors, and monitored entry points.

## A.7.2 Physical Entry Controls  
Access to secure areas must use:
- Badges  
- Keycards  
- Biometrics (if applicable)  
- Visitor logs  
Visitors must be escorted at all times.

## A.7.3 Securing Offices, Rooms, and Facilities  
Critical systems must be placed in locked rooms with limited access.  
Workstations must be positioned to prevent shoulder‑surfing.

## A.7.4 Protecting Against Environmental Threats  
Facilities must have:
- Fire suppression systems  
- Smoke detectors  
- Climate control (temperature/humidity)  
- Flood protection where applicable  

## A.7.5 Working in Secure Areas  
Only authorised personnel may work in secure zones.  
No personal devices allowed in server rooms.

## A.7.6 Delivery and Loading Areas  
Delivery zones must be separated from secure areas to prevent unauthorised access.

## A.7.7 Equipment Siting and Protection  
Servers and network devices must be:
- Mounted in locked racks  
- Protected from physical damage  
- Located away from public areas  

## A.7.8 Supporting Utilities  
Critical systems must have:
- Redundant power (UPS, generators)  
- Redundant cooling  
- Surge protection  

## A.7.9 Cabling Security  
Network and power cabling must be:
- Concealed or protected  
- Labelled  
- Shielded from interference  

## A.7.10 Equipment Maintenance  
Maintenance must be:
- Scheduled  
- Logged  
- Performed by authorised personnel only  

## A.7.11 Secure Disposal or Re‑use of Equipment  
Before disposal or reuse:
- Data must be securely wiped  
- Drives must be destroyed if wiping is not possible  
- Certificates of destruction must be retained  

## A.7.12 Unattended Equipment  
Users must:
- Lock screens when away  
- Secure laptops with cable locks in public areas  

## A.7.13 Clear Desk Policy  
Confidential documents must be stored securely when not in use.  
No sensitive data left on desks.

## A.7.14 Clear Screen Policy  
Screens must be locked when unattended.  
Auto‑lock enforced after 5 minutes.

---

## Summary  
A.7 ensures NovaPay’s physical environment is secure, resilient, and protected against unauthorised access, environmental threats, and equipment misuse.
