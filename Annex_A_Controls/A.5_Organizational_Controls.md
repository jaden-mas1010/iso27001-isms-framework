# Annex A.5 – Organizational Controls  
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.5 contains 37 organizational controls that define governance, responsibilities, policies, and operational structure for the ISMS.

---

## A.5.1 Policies for Information Security  
NovaPay maintains documented, approved, and communicated information security policies.

## A.5.2 Information Security Roles & Responsibilities  
Roles such as ISMS Manager, System Owners, and Asset Owners are formally defined.

## A.5.3 Segregation of Duties  
Critical tasks (e.g., deployment vs approval) are separated to reduce risk.

## A.5.4 Management Responsibilities  
Management ensures resources, support, and oversight for the ISMS.

## A.5.5 Contact with Authorities  
NovaPay maintains contact with regulators and law enforcement when required.

## A.5.6 Contact with Special Interest Groups  
Membership in ISACA, OWASP, and cybersecurity communities.

## A.5.7 Threat Intelligence  
Threat feeds integrated into SOC monitoring and risk assessments.

## A.5.8 Information Security in Project Management  
Security requirements included in all projects from initiation to closure.

## A.5.9 Inventory of Information & Assets  
Asset Register maintained (Asset_Register_NovaPay.xlsx).

## A.5.10 Acceptable Use of Assets  
Defined in Acceptable Use Policy.

## A.5.11 Return of Assets  
All assets must be returned during offboarding.

## A.5.12 Classification of Information  
Information classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted.

## A.5.13 Labelling of Information  
Confidential data must be labelled appropriately.

## A.5.14 Information Transfer  
Secure channels (TLS 1.2+) required for data transfer.

## A.5.15 Access Control  
Least privilege, RBAC, MFA enforced.

## A.5.16 Identity Management  
Unique IDs, strong authentication, lifecycle management.

## A.5.17 Authentication Information  
Password policy enforced (minimum 12 characters, complexity).

## A.5.18 Access Rights  
Quarterly access reviews performed.

## A.5.19 Physical Security Perimeters  
Restricted zones around sensitive areas.

## A.5.20 Physical Entry Controls  
Badges, CCTV, visitor logs.

## A.5.21 Securing Offices & Facilities  
Locked server rooms and controlled access.

## A.5.22 Protecting Against Threats  
Fire suppression, climate control, environmental protections.

## A.5.23 Equipment Security  
Servers placed in secure racks.

## A.5.24 Supporting Utilities  
Redundant power and cooling.

## A.5.25 Cabling Security  
Network cabling protected from interference and damage.

## A.5.26 Equipment Maintenance  
Scheduled servicing and patching.

## A.5.27 Secure Disposal  
Data wiped before disposal using certified methods.

## A.5.28 Information Security Event Reporting  
SOC escalation process for all events.

## A.5.29 Incident Management  
Incident Response Procedure followed.

## A.5.30 Business Continuity  
BCP maintained and tested annually.

## A.5.31 ICT Readiness for Continuity  
Failover systems and redundancy in place.

## A.5.32 Legal & Contractual Requirements  
Compliance with GDPR, DPA 2018, PCI DSS (if applicable).

## A.5.33 Intellectual Property Rights  
Licensing compliance for software and content.

## A.5.34 Privacy & Protection of PII  
DPIAs performed for high‑risk processing.

## A.5.35 Records Management  
Retention schedules defined and enforced.

## A.5.36 Monitoring & Review  
KPIs, audits, and continuous monitoring.

## A.5.37 Independent Review  
Annual ISMS audit performed by independent party.
