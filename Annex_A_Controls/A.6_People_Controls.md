# Annex A.6 – People Controls  
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.6 contains 8 controls focused on human resource security, onboarding, training, and employee responsibilities.

---

## A.6.1 Screening  
NovaPay performs background checks (identity, employment history, criminal record where legally allowed) before employment for roles handling sensitive information.

## A.6.2 Terms & Conditions of Employment  
Employment contracts include:
- Confidentiality obligations  
- Acceptable use requirements  
- Security responsibilities  
- Disciplinary consequences  

## A.6.3 Information Security Awareness, Education & Training  
All employees must complete:
- Mandatory annual security awareness training  
- Phishing simulations  
- Role‑specific security training for privileged users  

Training completion is logged and monitored.

## A.6.4 Disciplinary Process  
A formal disciplinary process exists for:
- Policy violations  
- Misuse of systems  
- Negligence leading to security incidents  

Consequences range from warnings to termination.

## A.6.5 Responsibilities After Termination or Change of Employment  
Upon termination or role change:
- Access is revoked immediately  
- Assets are returned  
- Confidentiality obligations continue indefinitely  

## A.6.6 Confidentiality or Non‑Disclosure Agreements  
NDAs are required for:
- Employees  
- Contractors  
- Third‑party suppliers with access to NovaPay data  

NDAs are reviewed periodically.

## A.6.7 Remote Working  
Remote working must follow:
- MFA for all remote access  
- Encrypted devices  
- Secure VPN  
- No local storage of sensitive data  
- Compliance with Acceptable Use Policy  

## A.6.8 Mobile Device Security  
Mobile devices must enforce:
- Full‑disk encryption  
- Auto‑lock after 5 minutes  
- No installation of unauthorised apps  
- Remote wipe capability  
- MDM (Mobile Device Management) controls  

---

## Summary  
A.6 ensures NovaPay’s people — employees, contractors, and third parties — understand and uphold their security responsibilities throughout the employment lifecycle.
