# Remediation Roadmap
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Continuous Improvement

This roadmap outlines prioritized actions required to achieve full ISO 27001 compliance.

---

## 1. High Priority (0–3 Months)

- Implement centralized logging and SIEM integration (A.8.7)
- Formalize secure development lifecycle (A.8.28)
- Automate vulnerability scanning and patch management (A.8.9)
- Strengthen supplier monitoring and review cycles (A.5.21)

---

## 2. Medium Priority (3–6 Months)

- Expand business continuity testing (A.5.29)
- Improve access review automation (A.5.18)
- Enhance awareness training frequency (A.6.3)

---

## 3. Low Priority (6–12 Months)

- Improve physical security documentation (A.7.x)
- Expand cryptographic key lifecycle management (A.8.12)
- Optimize change management workflow (A.8.10)

---

## 4. Tracking & Reporting

Progress is reviewed:
- Monthly by ISMS Manager
- Quarterly by Senior Management
- Annually during internal audit

All remediation evidence is stored in the ISMS repository.
