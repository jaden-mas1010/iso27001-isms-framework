# Executive Summary
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – ISMS Overview

NovaPay’s Information Security Management System (ISMS) has undergone a structured assessment aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The organization demonstrates strong governance, risk management, and operational security maturity.

Key strengths:
- Clear ISMS scope and documented policies
- Strong asset management practices
- Effective incident response workflow
- Supplier security controls implemented
- Business continuity and disaster recovery defined

Key gaps:
- Logging and monitoring (A.8.7) partially implemented
- Secure development lifecycle (A.8.28) requires formalization
- Supplier monitoring (A.5.21) needs improvement
- Technical vulnerability management (A.8.9) requires automation

Overall, NovaPay is progressing toward full ISO 27001 compliance with a clear roadmap for remediation.
