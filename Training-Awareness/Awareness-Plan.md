# Security Awareness Plan
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – A.6.3

NovaPay maintains a structured security awareness and training program to ensure all employees understand their responsibilities in protecting information assets.

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this plan is to:
- Educate employees on security risks
- Promote secure behaviour
- Reduce human-related security incidents
- Ensure compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022

---

## 2. Scope

This plan applies to:
- All employees
- Contractors
- Third-party personnel with access to NovaPay systems

---

## 3. Training Topics

Annual training covers:
- Phishing awareness
- Password hygiene
- Secure use of devices
- Data handling and classification
- Incident reporting
- Social engineering risks
- Secure remote working
- Supplier security awareness
- Business continuity basics

---

## 4. Delivery Methods

Training is delivered through:
- Instructor-led sessions
- Online modules
- Email campaigns
- Simulated phishing exercises
- Posters and internal communication

---

## 5. Frequency

- Mandatory annual training for all staff
- Quarterly micro-learning sessions
- Monthly phishing simulations
- Additional training after incidents or major changes

---

## 6. Tracking & Records

Attendance is recorded in **Attendance-Records.xlsx**.

Non-compliance triggers:
- Reminders
- Escalation to HR
- Temporary access restrictions (for critical systems)

---

## 7. Responsibilities

- **ISMS Manager:** Oversees program
- **HR:** Ensures participation
- **IT/SOC:** Provides technical content
- **Employees:** Complete required training

---

## 8. Review

This plan is reviewed:
- Annually
- After major incidents
- After changes to policies or threats
