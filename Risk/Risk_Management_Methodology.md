# Risk Management Methodology  
## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Clause 6.1

---

# 1. Purpose  
To define NovaPay’s methodology for identifying, assessing, treating, and monitoring information security risks in alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

---

# 2. Scope  
This methodology applies to:

- All NovaPay systems and applications  
- All cloud environments (AWS/Azure)  
- All employees and contractors  
- All suppliers and third‑party services  
- All information assets  

---

# 3. Risk Management Process  
NovaPay follows a 5‑step risk management process:

### 3.1 Identify Risks  
- Identify threats  
- Identify vulnerabilities  
- Identify impacted assets  
- Identify potential consequences  

### 3.2 Assess Risks  
Risk = Likelihood × Impact  
Both scored on a scale of 1–5.

### 3.3 Evaluate Risks  
Risk levels:

| Score | Level |
|-------|--------|
| 1–5 | Low |
| 6–12 | Medium |
| 13–25 | High |

High risks require immediate treatment.

### 3.4 Treat Risks  
Treatment options:

- Reduce (implement controls)  
- Avoid (stop activity)  
- Transfer (insurance, supplier)  
- Accept (with approval)  

### 3.5 Monitor Risks  
- Quarterly reviews  
- Updates after incidents  
- Updates after major changes  

---

# 4. Risk Criteria  

### Likelihood  
1 – Rare  
2 – Unlikely  
3 – Possible  
4 – Likely  
5 – Almost Certain  

### Impact  
1 – Negligible  
2 – Minor  
3 – Moderate  
4 – Major  
5 – Critical  

---

# 5. Risk Acceptance  
- Low risks: automatically accepted  
- Medium risks: accepted by ISMS Manager  
- High risks: require CEO approval  

---

# 6. Roles & Responsibilities  

### ISMS Manager  
- Maintains risk register  
- Oversees risk assessments  
- Ensures treatment plans are implemented  

### Department Heads  
- Provide asset information  
- Support treatment actions  

### Senior Management  
- Approves high‑risk acceptance  

---

# 7. Review  
This methodology must be reviewed:

- Annually  
- After major changes  
- Before certification audits
